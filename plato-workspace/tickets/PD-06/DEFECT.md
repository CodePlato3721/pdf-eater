
提问
when is Professor Friedrich Bhaer first appear

回答
Professor Friedrich Bhaer first appears in the book "Little Women" by Louisa May Alcott. He appears in the second part of the book, which is the third act, when he arrives at the March family home.

回答是错误的，该回答中没有 Professor Friedrich Bhaer
这段原文讲的是《小妇人》开头附近，March 姐妹自己排演的一出戏。里面的 Hagar、Hugo、Roderigo、Ferdinando 都是戏中戏的人物，不是《小妇人》主线里的真实人物；这里也完全没有出现 Professor Friedrich Bhaer。
